Product

This is a meeting intelligence platform.

The application takes meetings and converts them into structured, actionable intelligence.

Core workflow

Meeting → Transcript → AI Processing → Intelligence → Decisions → Action Items → Tasks → Follow-up

The product should help users answer:

What happened in the meeting?
What did we learn?
What was decided?
What needs to happen next?
Who is responsible?
What is overdue?
What patterns are emerging across meetings?
